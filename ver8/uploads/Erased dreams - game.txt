Erased dreams : game
Basic concept:
Base of the game: complete the dream and you will get the option to either erase it or keep it. Some of the dreams will help you. Some won't.  And the rest are just for fun.
Erase dream: permanently deletes the dream from the dreamer’s and and your own memory ( can affect story lines or relationships)
Keep dream: keeps it in memory - can unlock info, clues, or emotional connections later.
Each decision changes how the real world evolves


Game loop:
Explore real world: 
talk to characters, gather info about local mysteries or missing memories.
Access new “dream hosts” ( people whos dreams you can enter) 


Enter dream:
Explore surreal dream worlds in first person
Solve small puzzles or uncover symbolic clues
Interact with dream versions of people or new characters


Decision phase (end of dream)
A UI pops up “would you like to erase this dream or keep it?”
The choice changes the story, environment, and relationships


Progression:
Collect “dream fragments”
Unlock dream-world characters that can assist in both worlds
Solve bigger neighbourhood mysteries by piecing dream clues together
In the real world they could guide or warn you
Mechanics ideas:
Dream decay: some dreams fade automatically if ignored too long
Dual world interaction: changing something in the dream alters reality ( like a fire burning in someones room appearing in the real world)
NPC behaviour: dream choices might take NPCs act differently or even forget you exist


Dream journal system
This will be a physical item which will be a book that records every dream you’ve entered, your choice (keep/erase), and what you gained or lost from it.


Journal categories
Dream entries:
Each time you finish a dream it auto logs a new page in the journal


Displays:
Dream title- ( e.g. empty playground)
Dream summary- short description about the dream and what you experienced
Choice- kept or erased
Unlocks- characters, clues, or abilities gained
date/order: dream x (x = title)


Clues/ Mysteries tab
List of characters you’ve met with different signs
Dream world only 💤
Real world only 🌍
Both Worlds ⚡
